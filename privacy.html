Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

TIKTOKS is a content production and analytics application designed to help manage TikTok content.

Data we process

When a user connects a TikTok account, TIKTOKS may access profile information, public video information and statistical data authorized by the user through TikTok.

Purpose

This information is used to display account and video analytics inside the TIKTOKS dashboard.

Storage

TIKTOKS is currently designed as a local application. Application data is stored locally on the user's device unless otherwise stated.

Third-party services

TikTok data is accessed through TikTok's official developer APIs after user authorization.

Contact

Contact information will be provided by the application operator.
